# CS 5850 — Class-110: Final Project

This repository is your starting point for the **CS 5850 final project**. It is intentionally bare. Your first task is to copy a starting point into it from one of the classes you have already completed, and then evolve it into an original game of your own design.

> **Start thinking about your game now.** This repo is released early on purpose so you have weeks of background time to mull over mechanics, theme, and scope. You can begin designing as soon as you have read `narrative.md` — and you can revisit your starting-class choice as later classes are released. The Design Document (July 6) is when your choice has to be in writing; before then, treat it as provisional.

## Deliverables

Weights are **course grade** percentages from `2026/syllabus.md` (50% of the course total across these three). `rubric.md` scores each deliverable out of the **same number of points** (20, 10, 20).

| # | Deliverable | Due | Course grade weight |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Design Document (`submission/design.md`) | Mon, July 6, 2026 | **20%** (syllabus **Design Doc**) |
| 2 | Demo Video (uploaded to Canvas) | Mon, August 10, 2026 | **10%** (part of **Final Project**) |
| 3 | Final Game (`final` git tag, plus `submission/postmortem.md`) | Mon, August 18, 2026 | **20%** (part of **Final Project**) |

## Read in this order

1. **`narrative.md`** — what the final project is, what counts as "original," and how to copy a prior class into this repo as your starting point.

2. **`exercises.md`** — the full content spec for each of the three deliverables.

3. **`how-to-submit.md`** — the overview of how each deliverable is submitted.

4. **`how-to-submit-final.md`** — the comprehensive checklist for the August 18 final submission (build clean, tests pass, `final` tag, etc.). Use it the day before the deadline.

5. **`rubric.md`** — how each deliverable is graded.

## What's in `submission/`

The two stub files in `submission/` are templates. **Edit them in place** as you complete each deliverable:

- `submission/design.md` — fill in the eight required sections; due July 6.

- `submission/postmortem.md` — fill in the four required sections; due August 18.

The **demo video is submitted to Canvas**, not to this repo. See `how-to-submit.md` for the Canvas upload procedure.

## What about the LLM evaluation prompts?

Two LLM prompts ship with the repo:

- **`LLM-Evaluation-Prompt.md`** — the prompt the instructor runs against your design document to evaluate Deliverable 1. You may read it; it tells you what the grader is looking for.

- **`LLM-final-evaluation-prompt.md`** — the prompt the instructor runs against your final repository to evaluate Deliverable 3.

You are not required to run these prompts yourself, but reading them is good preparation.
